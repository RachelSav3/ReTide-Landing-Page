How it works

Three steps back on course.

Simple enough to use between sessions, with the information that matters front and centre.

01

Cancel a session

See the day's schedule and capacity. Move bookings from a cancelled session into a dedicated rebooking queue.

02

Find the next fit

View alternative sessions with enough places for the whole group, then move the booking to an available slot.

03

See what's at risk

Track bookings waiting to be rebooked, their euro value and alerts for bookings waiting over 48 hours.

What you can try today: These workflows are represented in the sample-data Atlantic Edge alpha. Dedicated kayaking and canoeing configurations, integrations and automated customer messaging are planned, not live features.
